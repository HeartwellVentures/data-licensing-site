The internet shows what businesses say. Your records show how they work.

AI systems were trained mostly on public writing. Public writing rarely shows the work itself: how a job gets quoted, how a schedule recovers when a crew calls out, how a complaint gets resolved.

You don't have to like AI to make a sound decision about it.

Licensed records are used to train AI systems to handle office work: scheduling, quoting, resolving claims. We won't tell you that's neutral, and some owners decide it isn't for them.

If you go ahead, you decide what is included, who may use it, and for what. Those limits are written into your agreement before anything leaves your systems. Declining costs you nothing.

Who this is for.

Usually a fit

  • 20 or more staff, now or at your peak.
  • Three or more years of records still on hand: email, files, operating systems.
  • Based in the US, with records mostly in English.
  • Operating, downsizing, winding down, or closed, as long as the records exist.

Not a fit, or not yet

  • Firms under 20 staff.
  • Records that are mainly patient health information. We don't arrange licenses for them.
  • Material you don't have the right to license, such as privileged client files. It is excluded, not licensed.

Questions owners ask first.

Something not covered here? Book a call and ask us directly.

Is this real money, or a pitch?

Real, and in writing before you commit. [BUYER] publishes licensing tiers starting at $100,000 for qualified firms. Your offer arrives as a written agreement with the price in it. You decide after reading it.

What does this cost me?

Nothing, at any stage. [BUYER] pays our fee when a deal closes. No deal, no fee.

You're paid by the buyer. Isn't that a conflict?

It's the same arrangement as a recruiter paid by the employer, and we disclose it here and in your agreement. Our work only pays if you sign, so our job is to bring you terms you're willing to sign. If they aren't right for you, there's no deal and no fee for anyone.

Do I give up my data?

No. You license a de-identified copy of the records you choose. Your originals, and your ownership of them, stay with you.

Will my clients or employees be exposed?

Names, contact details, account numbers, and credentials are replaced with labels before delivery, and you can exclude any records entirely. No method removes every risk, so you review the process and a sample before you sign.

Who will use the copy, and for what?

[BUYER], to build environments that train and evaluate AI systems on real business workflows. Your agreement names who may access the copy, what it may be used for, and whether the license is exclusive. [CONFIRM: downstream access by AI developers as permitted under the buyer agreement.]

Am I helping build something that replaces people like my team?

Possibly, in part. These records teach AI systems office work such as scheduling, quoting, and claims. [BUYER] already licenses records from other firms, so the decision in front of you is whether yours are included, and on what terms. Saying no costs nothing.

How much of my team's time does this take?

About an hour of yours. If an export is needed, we coordinate it with your IT person or provider. Nobody on your team creates new data or cleans anything up.

What if I don't know what records we have?

That's normal. Tell us which tools you've used over the years, and we'll work out what's there on the call.

Why now?

[BUYER] has announced its spending for the next twelve months, and the value sits in history you still hold. Archives get deleted when subscriptions lapse or a firm winds down. If you're unsure, a call costs nothing.
