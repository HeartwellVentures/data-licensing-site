# Pewter Data Funnel Teardown (reference)

Source pages saved by Jay at `workspace/user/files/`: home.html, deals.html
(identical to home), partners.html, refer.html. Full text extracts were made
Oct 13, 2026. What follows is the structural read: what they built, why it
works, what we take, what we reject.

## Funnel: companies
1. Hero: eyebrow ("For owners of US firms with 20+ employees"), H1 ("Your old
   email and files are worth six figures to AI labs. We handle the entire
   sale."), subhead with two specific deal examples ($350K accounting,
   $650K freight), trust strip ("No files to start · No cost to you · You keep
   your originals"), dual CTA (Get an offer / Or book a call).
2. Interactive payout estimator (employees + years sliders -> dollar range).
   The lead magnet is the funnel.
3. Proof stack: three recent payments, market context ($1B buyer commitment,
   $10M Spirit Airlines bid), Forbes CEO quote, filterable table of 15 "firms
   paid in 2026" ($110K-$1.2M).
4. Education: "The labs have read the internet. They have never seen how your
   business runs."
5. Before/after de-identification demo (original email vs de-identified copy).
6. Qualification checklist (20+ people, 3+ years, US, any status) + industry
   picker.
7. 8-question FAQ as objection handling.
8. Four-step close (request -> call -> written offer -> deliver/paid).
9. Offer form: name, firm, email, phone, employees, industry, consent,
   honeypot.

## Funnel: partners
1. H1: "Introduce business owners. Earn 10% of what they're paid." Subhead with
   cap ($100K per firm). Dual CTA (Become a partner / Register an introduction).
2. Interactive commission estimator.
3. "People owners already trust": CPAs, brokers/M&A, bankers/lenders/wealth,
   attorneys/consultants/IT.
4. Four steps: Apply -> Introduce -> We run the deal -> Get paid.
5. Application form + partner FAQ (pay timing, credit rules, permission,
   agreement + W-9).
6. Standalone referral form (/refer): referral code, business, owner contact,
   consent, 12-month credit.

## What we take (structure, not words)
- Dual funnel with dual estimators. The estimator-as-lead-magnet is the best
  conversion mechanic in the lane.
- Trust strip under the hero (three risk reversals, no scrolling needed).
- De-identification before/after demo. Non-negotiable; it is the trust anchor.
- FAQ as objection handling, placed late, written in the owner's voice.
- Honest caveats ("illustration only," "not independently verified," "no
  method removes every risk"). These convert skeptics and we keep the tactic
  with our own true statements.
- First-to-register 12-month credit rule for partners. Clean and fair.

## What we reject
- Their deal figures ($650K/$450K/$350K etc.). Self-reported claims about an
  unnamed "licensing partner." We never touch them.
- The anonymous payer model itself. Our buyer is named (Micro1) with verifiable
  facts.
- "We handle the entire sale" in the H1. We say license, always.
- No partner program exists on their site. That is the white space we occupy.

## Design notes (their aesthetic, for contrast)
Golds, blacks, beiges; Jay's read: "vibe coded, institutional." We go the
opposite direction: high-end corporate tech per the Glassnode reference.
Their information architecture is worth studying; their visual design is not.
