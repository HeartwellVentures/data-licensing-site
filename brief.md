# Master Brief

## 1. Positioning
We are the trusted guide that turns a company's existing records into licensing
income from AI labs. We are not a marketplace, not a tech platform, not a fund.
We are a deal firm: we originate, structure, and close data-licensing deals, and
we pay the people who bring them to us.

One-line: **We help established companies license their operational records to
AI labs, and we pay partners who make the introduction.**

## 2. Audiences (two, equal weight)
**A. Company owners.** US firms, 20+ employees, 3+ years of records (email, files,
operating systems). They are skeptical, busy, and have never heard of data
licensing. They care about: is this real money, what does it cost me, do I lose
my data, will my clients be exposed, how much of my time. They do not care about
AI. Speak to the money, the safety, and the simplicity.

**B. Partners.** CPAs, business brokers, M&A advisors, bankers, lenders, wealth
advisors, attorneys, consultants, IT providers. People business owners already
trust. They care about: how much do I earn per introduction, when do I get paid,
how do I get credit, do I need the owner's permission, is there a quota. They
want a second income stream from relationships they already have.

## 3. What we are not
- Not Pewter Data. Closest positioning comp, best funnel in the lane, but their
  paying "licensing partner" is anonymous and unverifiable. We win on named
  credibility (Micro1) and a real partner program (which they do not have).
- Not a consumer data play (Vana, Kled, Verb). We work with companies, not
  individuals.
- Not hype. Every number on the site must be verifiable. See "Verified numbers"
  in the copy deck.

## 5. Funnel architecture
**Company funnel:** Estimator (interactive, 30 seconds) -> "Get an offer" form
(name, firm, email, phone, employees, industry) -> we call within one business
day -> confidential call -> written offer -> de-identify, deliver, paid. Owner
time: about one hour total. Secondary CTA everywhere: "Or book a call."

**Partner funnel:** Commission estimator (interactive) -> "Become a partner" form
(name, firm, email, phone, profession, owners in network) -> referral code issued
immediately, agreement within one business day -> partner introduces via link or
referral form -> we run the deal, email updates at each stage -> partner paid
after the business is paid. No cost, no quota, no exclusivity.

**Lead magnets (built into the funnel, not downloads):**
1. Payout estimator (companies). 2. Commission estimator (partners).
3. Before/after de-identification demo (the single strongest trust asset).
4. Market proof stack (verifiable deals and commitments).
5. FAQ as objection handling (not a help page).

## 6. Trust architecture (in order of appearance)
1. Specific numbers with named sources (never round claims without attribution).
2. The de-identification demo: show exactly what leaves the building.
3. Honest caveats, stated plainly ("estimates are illustrations," "no method
   removes every risk"). Caveats convert skeptics.
4. Named buyer (Micro1) with verifiable facts ($1B commitment, Oct 2026).
5. Qualification criteria (saying who does NOT qualify makes the yes believable).
6. Risk reversal repeated: no cost at any stage, no files to start, walk away
   any time before signing, approve every term in writing.

## 7. Language rules
- **License**, never **sell**. "You keep your originals. You keep ownership."
- De-identified (US business audience), not anonymised.
- Plain sentences. If a sentence needs a comma splice to be understood, rewrite it.
- "We," never "I." Institutional voice throughout.
- No em dashes. No hype adjectives (revolutionary, game-changing, unlock).
- Never promise a result. Estimates are illustrations; offers come after review.
