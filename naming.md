# Naming

## Criteria
- Institutional and trustworthy. This is a trust business; the name must sound
  like a firm a 60-year-old owner would wire money through.
- Speaks to licensing, rights, or ownership. Never to "selling data."
- Short, spellable on a phone call, no clever spellings.
- Must stand next to Micro1, Troveo, Defined.ai without embarrassment.

## Shortlist (on merit)
1. **Provenant** — from provenance: clean origin, clean rights. The entire
   business is proving data is cleanly sourced and rights-cleared. Distinctive
   and institutional. Top pick.
2. **Titleline** — the title-insurance frame: we clear title to your data
   before it ships. Sophisticated, financial, memorable.
3. **Ownline** — "you keep ownership" in a single word. Punchy and ownable.
4. **Rightsline** — direct and legalistic. Clear, slightly cold.
5. **Ledgerline** — your records, kept and licensed. Slight crypto echo is the
   risk.
6. **Clearlicense** — descriptive and plain-spoken. Matches the common-person
   voice, less distinctive.
7. **Datakeep** — "keep your data." Simple to the point of generic.
8. **Fairshare** — the partner-split story inside the name. Warm; possibly too
   soft for the institutional bar.

## Inspiration from the space (Oct 13, 2026 survey)
Patterns that work for companies serving the models:
- **Rights-forward:** Rightsify (music datasets for AI), TollBit (the tollbooth
  for data). Lead with the right, not the data.
- **Double meaning:** Fairly Trained (fair + clearly). The craft to emulate.
- **Valuable collection:** Troveo (a trove). Maps to "your company's records."
- **Short invented:** Dawex, Pixta, Vana, Kled. Distinctive but says nothing;
  needs marketing to fill it.
- **Clear + noun:** Bright Data, Defined.ai. Plain-spoken trust.

New candidates from these patterns:
- **Grantline** — a grant is a conveyance of rights. "We grant licenses."
  Short, institutional, financial. (.com taken; needs modifier.)
- **Accord Data** — an accord is an agreement. Bank-like gravity. (.com taken.)
- **Clearline** — clean title + transparent dealing. Double meaning in the
  Fairly Trained spirit. (.com taken.)
- **Fairshare** (from shortlist) — echoed here deliberately: fair dealing +
  the partner split, same double-meaning craft.

## Domain reality check (Oct 13, 2026)
Every straightforward .com tested (provenant, ownline, rightsline, titleline,
ledgerline, clearlicense, datakeep, vaultline, licenseline, datatitle, and 10+
more) resolves, which usually means taken or parked. DNS resolving is not
proof of unavailability (many are parked and buyable), but expect the exact
.com to require either a purchase or a modifier.
